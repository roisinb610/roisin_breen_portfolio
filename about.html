About

I make big projects happen. For ten years that has meant shows, campaigns and brand collaborations in Paris fashion.

I'm Head of Production & Partnerships at Marine Serre. The job is part producer, part negotiator, part air-traffic control: budgets, legal clearance, international logistics, and getting a lot of different teams to land on the same day.

I've worked with the Louvre, Under Armour, Repetto and Make Up For Ever. I like clear deliverables, hard deadlines and a good spreadsheet.

Outside work I write, take photographs and draw.
