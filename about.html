About me.

I'm a producer and partnerships strategist. Across independent labels, luxury houses and cultural institutions, I've managed budgets from €5k to €700k, and raised over €200k in partnership funding to fund the campaigns, runway shows and boutique openings that follow.

I don't simply manage budgets; I build them. I negotiate them. I challenge them. I constantly look for opportunities to maximise creative ambition while protecting commercial objectives.

This dual perspective has given me a genuine 360° understanding of how creative projects are conceived, financed, produced and ultimately experienced.

I am constantly questioning how workflows, communication and production systems can evolve. I'm interested in building processes that don't simply improve one project, but strengthen every project that follows.
