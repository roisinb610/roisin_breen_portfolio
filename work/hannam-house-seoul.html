The project

A 2-year project delivering a 193m² flagship: Marine Serre's 1st standalone global flagship and 1st store outside Paris, in partnership with Musinsa Trading.

Opening at Kockiri, Itaewon. Guests: Rowoon, Natty, Lee Misook.

My role

Led the brand-side production from initial location search through concept, construction and opening, working with Musinsa Trading on local execution.

Managed the production of the campaign and architecture shoot used as teaser content ahead of launch, and sourced and negotiated the Uber Korea partnership directly.

Delivered branded transport for VIPs across the opening and after party, extending the partnership into the following week with private chauffeured access and a filmed interview with Marine Serre.

The result

159 check-ins against a target of 100. 369 total posts. +110% brand mentions following the event.

Press: Vogue Korea · Marie Claire Korea · Eyes Magazine · WWD · V Magazine · FashionNetwork.
