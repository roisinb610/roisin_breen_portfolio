Collaborations.

I manage collaborations end-to-end, from initial negotiation through final delivery. Structuring partnership terms & assembling and coordinating cross-functional teams: creative, legal, production, finance, marketing, and press.

Managing budgets across the full project lifecycle. Projects with international brands, cultural institutions, and artists, each with a different structure but the same requirements: aligning stakeholders early, keeping every workstream accountable, and coordinating delivery across multiple formats, from campaign and film production to retail activations and live events.

Priorities throughout are transparency, reliability, and ensuring the outcome serves both parties' objectives.
