The project

Marine Serre's 1st show outside Paris and 1st menswear runway, as Pitti Immagine Uomo's guest designer, at the 15th-century Villa di Maiano.

My role

Led the Pitti Immagine Uomo partnership alongside full international production, coordinating 200+ cast and crew from Paris and working with Without Production on technical delivery.

Produced the show, overseeing venue, travel, schedules, budgets, creative teams, technical production and guest experience. Also produced the post-show garden party and late-night event for 100+ guests.

The result

Marine Serre became Pitti Uomo's 106th guest designer, following previous guests including Raf Simons and Jean Paul Gaultier.

Coverage: WWD · AnOther · Hypebeast · Hypebae · Grazia · FashionNetwork.
