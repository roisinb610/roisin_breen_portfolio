SS25 Sempre Legati

Villa di Maiano, Fiesole · 12 June 2024 · Pitti Immagine Uomo.

Role

Venue
Villa di Maiano, Fiesole
Date
12 June 2024
Technical
Without Production

Firsts

1st Marine Serre show outside Paris. 1st menswear runway.

What I did

Led the Pitti Immagine Uomo partnership alongside full international production, coordinating 200+ cast and crew from Paris and working with Without Production on technical delivery.

Produced the show at the 15th-century Villa di Maiano, overseeing venue, travel, schedules, budgets, creative teams, technical production and guest experience. Also produced the post-show garden party and late-night event for 100+ guests.

The result

Marine Serre became Pitti Uomo's 106th guest designer, following previous guests including Raf Simons and Jean Paul Gaultier.
