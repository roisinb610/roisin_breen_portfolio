Marine Serre × Make Up For Ever

From backstage sponsor to a couture dress made of 850 brushes.

Client
Make Up For Ever
Brand
Marine Serre
Role
Partnership lead
Seasons
FW24–FW26

The brief

It started as a show sponsorship in FW24. Make Up For Ever covered artist fees, makeup teams, product and backstage support, and kept coming back: SS25, FW25, SS26.

What I did

I pushed the partnership past backstage and into couture. Make Up For Ever opened up their dead stock, and we turned it into a dress made of 850 makeup brushes.

I managed the selection and shipping of the brush samples.

The result

Esther Expósito wore the dress in the Louvre campaign. The couture extension also brought more product support and a bigger financial contribution from Make Up For Ever.
