The project

A collection of 24 pieces every season, across 3 contracted drops, with a campaign and a 3-day launch at Galerie Joseph.

My role

Full campaign production, shot by Nicola Delorme, starring Mathis Chevalier: 25+ crew and talent across film, video and print, cast, directed and delivered on schedule to brand, e-commerce, press and partners worldwide.

Launch: 3 days at Galerie Joseph.

Coordination of the collaboration terms from first discussion to realisation, coordinating all internal and external teams.

The result

1,000+ guests, VIPs and press on the night.
