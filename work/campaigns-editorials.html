The project

Each campaign built against a specific seasonal commercial objective: category strategy, retail growth, market positioning.

My role

Owning budget sign-off from €15K to €90K. Full ownership across every one: budget, talent and team negotiation and contracts, location scouting and negotiation, delivered under internal direction, whether run entirely in-house or in partnership with an external production company.

The result

Repeat collaborations with name-level talent and crews on a fraction of a typical campaign budget, through direct negotiation rather than agency mark-up.
