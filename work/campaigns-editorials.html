The project

Each campaign built against a specific seasonal commercial objective: category strategy, retail growth, market positioning.

My role

Budget sign-off from €5K to €150K, with full ownership across every one: budget, talent and team negotiation and contracts, location scouting and negotiation, delivered under internal direction, whether run entirely in-house or in partnership with an external production company.

The result

As comfortable with a large production and a team of up to 65 on set as with a smaller envelope, where the job is to be flexible and inventive with the money: negotiating directly, and bringing in deals, sponsorships and in-kind support so the work never looks like the budget.

Long-running collaborations with photographers, directors, stylists and casting directors, who come back season after season.
