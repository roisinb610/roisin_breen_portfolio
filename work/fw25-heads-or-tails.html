The project

A collision of two worlds, the institutional weight of La Monnaie de Paris and the cinematic universe of David Lynch, merged into one coherent narrative. Twin Peaks' red room, Laura Palmer, and a procession of femmes fatales, staged inside a national mint.

411 guests. 50 models. Film directed by Tess Lochanski, produced in partnership with Iconoclast.

My role

Full show production, in partnership with Spotlite. Led location research, scouting, site visits and negotiation to secure La Monnaie de Paris.

Partnerships negotiated directly: financial and product support from Make Up For Ever, Manucurist and Bumble and bumble.

The result

Reviewed by Suzy Menkes, Imran Amed and Angelo Flaccavento. Coverage across Vogue France, L'Officiel, Marie Claire, Numéro and Fashion Network.

A viral moment: a dress built entirely from watches. 900K+ views on TikTok, 6,000+ new followers, and a press conference that turned into a season-long run of inbound press and VIP requests.
